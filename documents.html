What documents to send us

The documents we need, depending on your situation. You can print it or save it as a PDF.

CIS and self-employed

  • Your UTR and National Insurance number
  • All monthly CIS statements for the tax year
  • Bank statements for the whole year
  • Invoices and receipts for tools, equipment and protective clothing
  • A mileage log (date, destination, miles)
  • Phone and internet bills
  • Other income: P60 or P45 from an employer, rent

Limited company

  • Company bank statements, monthly
  • Sales invoices
  • Purchase invoices and receipts
  • Lease or loan agreements
  • Payroll records, if we don't run your payroll
  • Your Companies House authentication code
  • Company UTR and VAT number, if you have one

New company (setup)

  • Your preferred company name, plus 1–2 alternatives
  • Registered office address
  • For each director: name, date of birth, nationality, address
  • Each director's National Insurance number
  • Valid photo ID for Companies House identity verification
  • What the company does
  • Shareholders and number of shares
