Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

We take the protection of your data seriously. This page explains what data we collect, why, how long we keep it and your rights under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who we are

Servicii De Contabilitate Anglia, London, United Kingdom, is the controller of your personal data. Contact us at serviciidecontabilitateanglia@gmail.com or +44 7909 451914.

What data we collect

  • When you contact us by form, e-mail, WhatsApp or phone: your name, phone number, e-mail address, your situation and your message. Messages sent through the website form are delivered to our e-mail by the Web3Forms service.
  • When you book a consultation through Calendly: your name, e-mail address, the time you choose and anything you write in the booking form.
  • When you become a client: the data needed for your accounts and tax returns, such as your UTR and National Insurance number, income, expenses and identity documents.
  • The calculators on this site run only in your browser. The figures you enter are not sent to us or stored.

Why we use your data and our legal basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote: steps before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) UK GDPR).
  • To provide the services you engage us for: performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • To meet our legal obligations, such as keeping accounting records and anti-money laundering checks (Article 6(1)(c)).

How long we keep your data

  • Enquiries that don't lead to working together: up to 12 months.
  • Client data: for as long as we work together, then as long as the law requires, usually 5 to 6 years after the relationship ends.

Who we share data with

We never sell or rent your data. We only share it:

  • with HMRC and Companies House, when you authorise us to act for you;
  • with the providers we use for communication and hosting: Google (Gmail and Google Meet), WhatsApp (Meta), Calendly (bookings), Web3Forms (contact form) and GitHub (website hosting). They may process data outside the UK with the safeguards the law requires.

GitHub's hosting may record IP addresses in technical logs for security.

Cookies and statistics

This site does not use tracking or advertising cookies. For visitor statistics we use GoatCounter, which uses no cookies, does not store IP addresses and does not identify you. We only see the number of visits, pages viewed, country and device type.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected or erased, restrict or object to its processing, and receive it in a portable format. E-mail us at the address above and we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk, phone 0303 123 1113.
