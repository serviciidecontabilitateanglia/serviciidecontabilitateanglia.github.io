FAQ

Questions? We have answers.

What is a UTR and do I need one?

A UTR (Unique Taxpayer Reference) is a 10-digit number from HMRC. You need one if you are self-employed or have to file a Self Assessment tax return. We can register you.

When is the Self Assessment deadline?

The UK tax year runs from 6 April to 5 April the following year. Online returns must be filed by 31 January after the tax year ends. HMRC charges a £100 penalty for late filing, which increases over time.

I work in construction under CIS. Can I get money back?

In many cases, yes. CIS payments usually have 20% deducted. Once allowable expenses are claimed (tools, travel, protective equipment and so on), many clients get part of that money back.

What is Making Tax Digital and does it affect me?

Making Tax Digital (MTD) means keeping digital records and sending quarterly updates to HMRC through compatible software. It already applies to all VAT-registered businesses. Since April 2026 it also applies to self-employed people and landlords with income over £50,000, and the threshold falls in the coming years.

Should I be self-employed or set up a limited company?

It depends on how much you earn and the type of work you do. We look at your situation and tell you which option works best for you.

I received a letter from HMRC and don't understand it. Can you help?

Yes. Send us a photo of the letter on WhatsApp and we'll explain what it means and what needs to be done.

Can you help me get a mortgage or remortgage?

We prepare the documents your lender or broker asks for: SA302s and Tax Year Overviews, annual accounts and an accountant's income letter. The pack starts from £200. We don't give mortgage advice or arrange mortgages. To choose a mortgage, we recommend an FCA-authorised broker.

Do you only work with clients in London?

No. We're based in London but work 100% online with clients across the UK. You send documents by e-mail or WhatsApp, and we hold consultations by phone or video.
