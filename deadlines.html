Tax deadlines

Never miss an HMRC deadline

Key dates for self-employed people, CIS subcontractors and Making Tax Digital. Add them to your calendar to get a reminder.

Next deadline: ,

  1. 5 Oct 2026

    Register for Self Assessment

    If you started working for yourself (including under CIS) in the 2025/26 tax year, you must register with HMRC by this date.

  2. 31 Oct 2026

    2025/26 paper tax return

    Last day for paper Self Assessment returns. Online returns are due by 31 January.

  3. 7 Nov 2026

    MTD quarterly update

    For 6 July – 5 October 2026, if you're in Making Tax Digital (income over £50,000).

  4. 30 Dec 2026

    Pay through your PAYE tax code

    If you're also employed and owe less than £3,000, file online by today to have it collected through your wages.

  5. 31 Jan 2027

    2025/26 online tax return and payment

    Final deadline to file online and pay your tax, plus your first payment on account for 2026/27. Filing late means a £100 penalty.

  6. 7 Feb 2027

    MTD quarterly update

    For 6 October 2026 – 5 January 2027.

  7. 5 Apr 2027

    End of the 2026/27 tax year

    The tax year closes. Start gathering your records for the next return.

  8. 7 May 2027

    MTD quarterly update

    For 6 January – 5 April 2027.

  9. 31 Jul 2027

    Second payment on account for 2026/27

    Your second payment on account, if HMRC asked you to make them.
